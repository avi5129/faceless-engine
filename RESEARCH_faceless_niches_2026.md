## Source 1 — ReelPilot "Faceless YouTube RPM by Niche in 2026"
URL: https://reelpilot.app/blog/faceless-youtube-rpm-by-niche (June 21, 2026)
RPM = revenue per 1,000 MONETIZED views (after YouTube's 45% cut). Blended
Shorts+long-form estimate. 15x spread top-to-bottom for the SAME view count.

RPM by niche, ranked (2026):
  Personal Finance & Investing   $12–22   Saturation: High
  Business & Entrepreneurship    $9–18    Medium
  Tech & AI                      $7–15    High
  Education & How-To             $6–13    Medium
  Health & Fitness               $6–12    Medium
  History & Documentary          $4–9     Low
  Motivation & Mindset           $3–8     High
  True Crime & Mystery           $3–7     Medium
  Entertainment & Stories        $2–6     High
  ASMR & Satisfying              $1.20–4  Medium

Key point from source: "a million views in the wrong category can pay less than
a hundred thousand in the right one." Shorts pay far less per view than
long-form mid-rolls (shared ad pool). RPM swings by geography (US/UK/CA/AU pay
multiples of tier-3) and season (Q4 >> January).

## Source 2 — NicheRoza "12 Best Faceless YouTube Niches in 2026"
URL: https://nicheroza.com/en/blog/best-faceless-youtube-niches-2026 (May 29, 2026)
Winning-niche test (their 3 criteria): healthy demand + real outlier potential
(small channels can still break out) + solo-friendly production.

Top 12:
  1. AI-Generated Story Channels (sci-fi, alternate-history "what if", serial fiction)
  2. History & Documentary (durable, high watch time, ages slowly = earns for years)
  3. Stoic Philosophy & Self-Improvement (evergreen, loyal audience, low cost)
  4. Money Psychology & Personal Finance (RPM often >$9; competition real)
  5. Sleep Stories & Relaxation (low RPM, huge watch time)
  6. Space & Science Explainers (curiosity demand + strong RPM; Shorts+long-form)
  7. Tech Reviews & Explainers (best RPMs, high purchase intent)
  8. Mythology & Folklore Animation (evergreen, rarely saturated at small-channel level)
  9. True Crime Narration (engaged audience; accuracy-sensitive)
  10. "Top 10" & List Channels (repeatable, batch-friendly, chase high-RPM angles)
  11. Personal Development & Productivity
  12. Health & Wellness Explainers (strong RPM, trust-driven, accuracy-sensitive)

## Cross-source agreement
- HIGH RPM, faceless-feasible, solo-friendly: Finance, Tech/AI, History/
  Documentary, Space/Science, True Crime.
- Your CURRENT niche ("curiosity / did-you-know facts") is NOT on either
  high-RPM list. It lands near "Entertainment & Stories" ($2–6) at best, and
  competes directly with the most saturated, lowest-RPM, highest-swatch-away
  corner of Shorts. This matches the earlier viewer-test verdict: in this niche
  a weak visual = instant swipe = zero cents.
- Both sources stress: niche matters MORE than upload volume; a format that
  "looks different in the feed" beats more uploads in a saturated niche.

## What this means for the engine (FACT, not opinion)
The engine already produces: Chatterbox voice, gold karaoke captions, licensed
visuals, 2.5–4s restrained cuts. That pipeline serves EVERY niche above — it is
niche-AGNOSTIC. The only niche-specific input is the SCRIPT + the visual query
ladder + topics.txt. So switching wedge = editing topics.txt + script tone,
NOT rebuilding the engine. The visual-floor defect (gradient-only fallback)
must still be fixed because it kills retention in ANY niche.
