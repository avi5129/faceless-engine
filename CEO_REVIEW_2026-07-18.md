# Independent CEO / Strategy Review — faceless-engine

Verdict up front: the plan optimizes a process that does not yet earn, and
contradicts its own office-hours discipline. The "earn in 30 days, no manual
work" goal is internally inconsistent and most of its revenue math is unsound.

## 1. Is this the right problem?
No. "No manual work" is false: P3 (manual publish), manual upload_map.json, and
unresolved D1 (revenue link) all require the human. The defensible product is
not a money-printing channel — it is the pipeline itself, sold to the millions
chasing faceless income. Reframe: (a) validate the channel honestly as a manual
publish test, OR (b) pivot to selling the engine/course. Today it claims both
and delivers neither.

## 2. Premises assumed, not proven
- "History niche earns" → History RPM is $4–9 (LOW); the switch from curiosity
  barely moves the needle and is still demand-unproven.
- "Off-platform revenue in 30 days" → requires an audience first. Cold channel
  + description-link affiliates = near-zero conversions.
- "Scheduler = hands-off" → it generates; human still publishes and maps uploads.
- "Wikimedia fixes gradient" → build_beat_visual STILL falls to gradient as the
  last resort (F3 open); license filter can over-reject history imagery.

## 3. Six-month regret
90 days of auto-generated + manually published videos to a dead cold channel,
$0 realized, momentum spent — while the engine could have been the product. The
30-day window closes with zero revenue and the project dies of disappointment.

## 4. Dismissed alternatives
- Office-hours REJECTED "keep building" (Approach B). This PLAN is exactly that:
  4 waves of gstack gates (autoplan, plan-eng, plan-design, plan-devex) = process
  theater that delays the only thing that matters (viewer signal).
- Selling the tool/SaaS/course to faceless aspirants = faster, real revenue;
  not analyzed.
- Buying an aged/monetized channel to skip the YT threshold; not considered.
- Long-form as the affiliate载体 (tappable descriptions) under-explored.

## 5. Competitive / market risk
Faceless AI is the MOST saturated corner of YouTube; the research itself cites
dozens of "40+ niches" videos — proof thousands are doing this. First-mover is
gone. A one-GPU hobby pipeline loses on quality to funded teams. Differentiation
is absent.

## Findings (what's wrong / severity / fix)
F1 CRITICAL "No manual work" false. Fix: re-scope as auto-generate + you publish.
F2 CRITICAL Shorts description/pinned links are nearly invisible (not tappable in
   Shorts feed). The whole 30-day off-platform thesis rests here. Fix: bio link /
   link-in-bio page + short URL in comment; or lead with long-form.
F3 CRITICAL Live scheduler already ships the REJECTED output: niche=curiosity,
   kokoro, no Pexels key → template scripts + 100% gradient = swipe-past. F3/F7
   still open. Fix: halt scheduler; fix visual floor + wedge first.
F4 HIGH 30-day conversion math fails: cold channel, ~90 Shorts, most <100 views;
   1 conversion needs ~5–10k qualified views → realistically 0 by day 30. Fix:
   redefine success as retention/view signal, not conversion.
F5 HIGH "History earns" is a weak, unproven premise. Fix: treat wedge as a
   testable hypothesis, not a locked decision.
F6 HIGH Plan bloats into gstack gates, contradicting office-hours. Fix: run the
   30-day publish test now; gate features on real signal.
F7 MEDIUM Gradient fix not in code; license filter may over-reject. Fix: ship a
   cached local licensed image pool before resuming.
F8 MEDIUM Caption hard-gate raises RuntimeError → whole run dies if stable-ts/
   venv missing, contradicting "never dies." Fix: graceful beat-level fallback.
F9 MEDIUM Saturation/competitive. Fix: micro-wedge with a personal angle.
F10 MEDIUM Reused/licensed + AI-voice may trip YT reuse/inauthentic policies,
   hurting later monetization. Fix: confirm policy posture pre-scale.
